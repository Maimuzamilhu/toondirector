ToonDirector privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

ToonDirector is a Windows app that makes animated videos on your PC. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The developer does not run a server for the app and does not receive your data, unless you send a report yourself.

What stays on your PC

Your videos, voice recordings, settings and API keys are stored in the data folder you choose. Voice conversion, speech recognition, face tracking and video drawing all run on your PC. Your voice recordings and video files are not uploaded by the app.

API keys are stored without encryption in that folder, so keep it private. Uninstalling the app can leave this folder in place. You can delete it at any time.

What leaves your PC

The app sends data only to the services needed for what you ask it to do:

  • The AI providers you connect. Your topics, scripts, directions and research notes are sent to them. If you turn a video into a cartoon, still frames from that video, which can show people's faces, are also sent. Each provider handles this data under its own privacy policy, and some free services may use it to improve their models. You choose which providers to use.
  • Microsoft's text-to-speech service. Your script is sent to it to create the narration.
  • Wikipedia. Search terms for your topic are sent to it for research.
  • Tavily, only if you add a Tavily key. Your topic is sent to it for web research.
  • YouTube, only if you paste a video link. The link is sent to YouTube's public oEmbed address to read the video's title. Nothing is downloaded from YouTube.
  • Download servers, when you choose an add-on. They can see your IP address, as any website can.

Voices of other people

Only use a recording of your own voice, or of someone who has given you permission. The app asks you to confirm this. Recordings are used to change the narration's voice on your PC and stay in that video's folder.

Reports

If you use Report a problem, your email app opens a message to the developer. It includes your app version and, if you started it from a video, the video's title. You can read and edit it before sending. The developer uses reports to respond to you and to fix problems.

Your choices

You can remove a key, turn off a provider or delete a video inside the app. Deleting the data folder removes everything the app has stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
